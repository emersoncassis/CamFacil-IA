# AI training for CCTV

A short course in the browser, nothing to download. Narrated by someone who trained installers all over Brazil.

!!! info "Language"
    The course and its narration are currently in Portuguese.

[Start the course](https://cfia.ysafe.com.br/aprender){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## How it works

- **Six lessons** with hands-on exercises: you draw the line and the zone, and the AI tells you if you got it right.
- **Got it wrong?** The voice explains why and you try again.
- **IP networking too:** what an IP is, what a network conflict is and why a camera disappears.

## Completion badge

When you finish, you sign in with Google and generate a **badge with your details**, ready to share on LinkedIn, Instagram and WhatsApp. If you add your Instagram, you may be reposted in the stories.
