<details class="parte" open>
<summary data-n="01">Video AI belongs to the installer, too</summary>
<p>The one who climbs the ladder, runs the cable, crimps the connector and focuses the camera is the installer. The one who answers the customer at ten at night because the recorder stopped is the installer. Even so, when the talk turned to artificial intelligence, people started treating it as something for labs, big manufacturers or integrators with a team of engineers.</p>
<p>It isn't. Basic video AI, the kind that solves everyday problems, can be learned. Line crossing, virtual fence, loitering zone, camera health. It has rules, and those rules fit in the head of any installer who has ever done a clean job.</p>
</details>
<details class="parte">
<summary data-n="02">Why this exists</summary>
<p>CamFácil IA exists to set the installer free: free from depending on whoever keeps the knowledge, from the fear of offering something you don't master, and from the idea that you must spend a lot before proving anything.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Free as in freedom, and free of charge.</strong> Open source (AGPL-3.0). Nobody can take it away from you.</li>
<li><strong>Runs on what you already have.</strong> An old PC, a TV box, a phone sitting in a drawer. No expensive server, no mandatory cloud.</li>
<li><strong>Teaches by doing.</strong> The course is short, in the browser, with real exercises. Get it wrong and the voice explains why.</li>
<li><strong>The customer is yours.</strong> Data stays on the customer's machine. Video goes straight to your phone, through no one else.</li>
</ul>
</details>
<details class="parte">
<summary data-n="03">Trust first, investment later</summary>
<p>You install it, prove it works and show value at almost zero cost. Once the customer believes in you, that's the time to step up: a camera with edge AI, an AI recorder, a commercial platform. CamFácil IA is the bridge that takes the installer there, with knowledge and with a customer in hand.</p>
</details>
<details class="parte">
<summary data-n="04">This place is ours</summary>
<p>Computer vision will be in every camera in the coming years. The question is who will sell, install and maintain it. I want it to be the installer. Not as labor for those who know, but as the one who knows.</p>
<p>If this makes sense to you, start with the course and share it with another installer. That's how we take this place.</p>
<p class="assinatura"><strong>Emerson de Carvalho Assis</strong> · creator of CamFácil IA, former technical instructor in electronic security · #sangueverde</p>
</details>
