# Privacy and LGPD

!!! warning "A learning and validation environment"
    CamFácil IA is for study and validation, with no warranty. The final solution at the customer's site is a camera or recorder with edge AI, or a professional platform. The project **does not collect or host images**: the installer operates it and is responsible for data protection (Brazil's LGPD) with the customer.

## What the software already does for you

- Data stays **on the customer's machine**.
- **LGPD mode:** faces are blurred in anything that leaves the machine.
- Configurable **retention periods** for clips, the Telegram archive and studio photos.
- **Face recognition** is never on by default and is blocked in studio mode.
- Every terms acceptance is logged with date, time and version.
